Intervention playground

Force the activation of a feature to see how it influences the text generated by the model

How to use the playground

  1. Enter a prompt like I was driving when
  2. Choose the feature that you want to forcefully activate. Open the feature dropdown and scroll or search by ID or title.
  3. Choose how strongly to activate the feature:
    • 50% mildly activated
    • 100% maximum observed activation
    • 200% twice the maximum (may break the model)
  4. Press Generate comparison
